> **Rules applied:** citation keys only from §4; tier = the tier of the underlying claim being taught; no myths, no dopamine-hack framing, no outcome promises, no shame hooks (§10). Concepts intentionally recur *across* platforms (repurposing); each entry is a distinct execution, and no idea repeats within a platform.
> **Tag legend recap (§9):** `[THEME|Tier]` — themes: EF executive function · MO money · PL planning · HO home · HA habits · TB time blindness · DF decision fatigue · OR organization · PW product walkthroughs · RE research explanations. Tiers: T1 clinically supported · T2 moderately supported/indirect · T3 experimental · T4 unsupported; `T4-debunk` = the idea *debunks* a T4 claim. Problem-evidence claims (documented deficits, §4D/§4E) are tagged T1 when meta-analytic/registry-level adult evidence, T2 when single-study or extrapolated.
