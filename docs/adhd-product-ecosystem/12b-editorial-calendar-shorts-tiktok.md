Every idea teaches one evidence-based concept or an honest product angle; research-explanation (RE) lines name a §4 citation key from [00-evidence-foundation.md](00-evidence-foundation.md); no myths, no dopamine framing, no shame hooks, no outcome promises.
**Tag legend (per §9):** `[THEME|Tier]` — EF executive function · MO money · PL planning · HO home · HA habits · TB time blindness · DF decision fatigue · OR organization · PW product walkthrough · RE research explanation. Tiers: T1 clinically supported · T2 moderately/indirectly supported · T3 experimental · T4 unsupported/refuted (taught as "this doesn't work").
