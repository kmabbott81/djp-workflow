# 12c — Editorial Calendar: Instagram Reels & Carousels

> Part of the Anchor editorial system: 400 Instagram ideas — 200 Reels, 200 Carousels — each teaching one evidence-based concept or an honest product/walkthrough angle, traceable to [00-evidence-foundation.md](00-evidence-foundation.md).
> Voice per §8: a calm friend who has read the research. No myths, no dopamine-hack framing, no fake neuroscience, no shame hooks, no outcome promises; every research-explanation (RE) idea names a §4 citation key.
> Reels lean aesthetic-calm demos, voiceover explainers, and silent-text walkthroughs; Carousels are save-worthy teaching assets — the save is the metric.

**Tag legend recap (§9):** `[THEME|Tier]` — EF executive function · MO money · PL planning · HO home · HA habits · TB time blindness · DF decision fatigue · OR organization · PW product walkthrough · RE research explanation. Tiers: T1 clinically supported · T2 moderately supported/indirect · T3 experimental · T4 unsupported/refuted — **a T4 tag marks an idea that debunks an unsupported claim, never one that promotes it.** Named recurring series: Quiet Demo [PW] · Study in 30 Seconds [RE] · The Evidence Files [RE] · Body Double With Me [EF] · Sunday Reset [PL] · Time, Made Visible [TB].
